# Adobe Stock Contributor Facts — Official Sources Only

Last verified: 2026-10-31

> Disclaimer: Seasonal recommendations are research-based planning guidance. Adobe Stock download demand, ranking and sales performance are not guaranteed. No private Adobe Stock sales data is referenced here.

## 1. Photo submission specs
- Format: JPEG with sRGB color profile.
- Resolution: 4MP–100MP (megapixels).
- Maximum file size: 45MB.
- Content must be free of watermarks, timestamps, or branding; sharp focus, well-lit.
- Source: https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-photos/technical-legal-requirements-photo-submission.html (last updated Jun 11, 2026)

## 2. Vector / illustration requirements
- Accepted formats: AI (Adobe Illustrator), EPS (Encapsulated PostScript), SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics).
- Vector files saved in ZIP folders or uploaded as JPEGs are NOT accepted.
- EPS files should be exported at 15MP or more, or with "High Resolution" Transparency Presets for optimal preview quality; EPS files larger than 15MP can export Transparency Presets at 72 PPI to remain under the 45MB maximum.
- Customers receive an auto-generated JPEG preview plus the original EPS/AI/SVG; transparent/flat-color backgrounds get an auto-generated transparent PNG — contributors do not upload it separately.
- Sources: https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/help/vector-requirements.html and https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-vectors/technical-requirements-for-vector-submissions.html

## 3. Video submission basics
- Accepted formats: MOV, MP4, MPG, AVI.
- Duration: 5–60 seconds (10–30 seconds recommended for vertical).
- Maximum file size: 3900MB (3.9GB).
- Upload at the native frame size — do not up- or down-convert. Listed accepted resolutions include 1920×1080, 2048×1080, 3840×2160, 4096×2160, 4096×2304 (horizontal) and 1080×1920, 2160×3840, 2160×4096, 2304×4096 (vertical); minimum cited 1280×720.
- Submissions go through the Contributor Portal with descriptive metadata and required releases.
- Sources: https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/help/video-requirements.html ; https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2016/09/28/getting-started-in-stock-photography

## 4. Generative AI disclosure requirements
- The "Created using generative AI tools" checkbox is REQUIRED for all content created with generative AI software — always, regardless of whether a release is required.
- "People and Property are fictional" checkbox: select when AI content visually resembles people/property but is not based on a real person (then no release is required); leave unchecked if there are no recognizable people or property.
- Label content as generative AI when generative AI tools in Photoshop or Illustrator changed, augmented, or added a new primary subject (e.g., new person/animal/object, significant adjustments to a human subject, recoloring the primary subject). Labeling is NOT required for background extension, removing IP/copyrighted designs, retouching out distractions, or recoloring the background.
- Prohibited in generative AI prompts, titles, and keywords: names of artists, real people, or fictional characters; references to creative works still in copyright; names of government agencies; references to third-party IP; descriptions implying the content depicts an actual newsworthy event. Violations may result in content removal or account termination.
- Contributors must have the rights under their AI tool's terms to submit the content for commercial licensing (per the Contributor Agreement).
- AI video: mark as Created using generative AI; meet the same technical and legal requirements as traditional videos (including releases); avoid embedded text, signature watermarks, and model inconsistencies; do not replicate other artists' styles or use prompts tied to real-world IP.
- AI content depicting, based on, or intended to portray an identifiable real person requires a model release; real property requires a property release.
- Sources: https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/help/generative-ai-content.html ; https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-generative-ai-content/generative-ai-video-submission-guidelines.html

## 5. Metadata rules
- Keywords: up to 50 keywords, ordered by relevance (concepts, settings, demographics, locations, technical terms). The first 10 keywords are prioritized in search results.
- Titles: clear, accurate, descriptive; avoid ALL CAPS, brand names, and artist names.
- Titles, keywords, releases, and visuals must accurately represent the subject matter; use the correct language setting in the Contributor portal.
- For vectors: if no title or embedded keywords are present, the portal auto-suggests a title and up to 25 keywords (review, reorder, remove as needed).
- Sources: https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/get-started/become-contributor/tips-contributors.html (last updated Jun 11, 2026) ; https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/content-policies-guidelines/metadata/keywords-metadata-submitting-vectors.html

## 6. Similar-content / spam policy
- Similar content is one of the top reasons Adobe Stock refuses submissions.
- Be selective: submit only the strongest, most distinctive variations; diversify metadata — reusing identical titles/keywords on similar content can lead to refusal.
- Content is "too similar" when it matches in: subject and composition (same subject, slightly different angles/distances), creative treatment (identical concepts with only minor color/filter/effect changes), series submissions (sequential shots from the same moment/location with minimal meaningful difference), or metadata patterns (different images with identical titles/keywords).
- Adobe's help states the team accepts up to three color, style, and composition variations of the same image. Adobe's spam-block guidance: limit yourself to three significant versions of a single motif; croppings, mirrored images, duplicates (e.g., B&W then color), and minimal-difference submissions are prohibited; repeated violations can result in re-blocking or termination of the contributor account.
- Sources: https://helpx.adobe.com/content/help/id/en/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-distinct-content/distinct-content-submission-best-practices.html (last updated Sep 2, 2026) ; https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/help/artist-hub-migration/optimize-your-production-process.html

## 7. IP / trademark / logo restrictions
- Prohibited in commercial submissions: copyrighted logos, signage, and trademarked products — branded elements, company logos, and trademarked designs must be removed or avoided (partial removal is not an acceptable substitute per Adobe's stated guidance).
- Never include logos, brand names, or protected designs unless you own the rights.
- Recognizable artwork or murals (street art, sculptures): location-based IP that may require property releases or be prohibited entirely.
- Certain architectural landmarks with restricted rights are not acceptable — consult Adobe's known image restrictions list: https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/help/known-image-restrictions.html
- Sources: https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-photos/technical-legal-requirements-photo-submission.html ; https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-pngs/legal-requirements-png-submission.html (last updated Jun 11, 2026) ; https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/get-started/become-contributor/tips-contributors.html

## 8. Model release & property release basics
- Model release required: for any identifiable/recognizable person. Extends to recognizable voices in videos, and illustrations/vectors based on real people or body parts.
- Property release required: for recognizable private property, identifiable locations, artwork, or distinctive objects; recognizable property or ticketed locations (museums, stadiums, etc.) generally need one.
- Generative AI: content depicting, based on, or intended to portray an identifiable real person requires a model release; real property requires a property release. If people/property are fictional, use the "People and Property are fictional" checkbox instead.
- For minors, a parent or legal guardian must sign the release.
- Sources: https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-photos/technical-legal-requirements-photo-submission.html ; https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/help/generative-ai-content.html ; https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/get-started/become-contributor/tips-contributors.html
